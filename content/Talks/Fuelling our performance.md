> 1. Obvious
> 2. Attractive
> 3. Easy
> 4. Satisfying

> [!Quote] Productivity comes as a result of continuous learning.

> [!Question] How can we find time & space for learning every day?
> Follow the principles mentioned in [[Atomic Habits]],
> 1. Make it obvious
> 	- e.g., Tie the problem space you are working on with the learning objective
> 2. Make it attractive
> 	- e.g., Have a personal connection to the problem you are trying to solve?
> 3. Make it easy
> 	- e.g., Know when to stop, and move on.
