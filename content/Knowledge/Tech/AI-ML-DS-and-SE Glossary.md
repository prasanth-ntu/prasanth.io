| Regularization and Regularization Rate ($\lambda$) | One approach to keeping a model simple is to penalize complex models; that is, to force the model to become simpler during training. Penalizing complex models is one form of regularization.<br><br>i.e., Training optimization algorithm $minimise(loss + complexity)$<br><br>A regularization rate (lambda) controls the strength of regularization, with higher values leading to simpler models and lower values increasing the risk of overfitting.<br>i.e., $minimise(loss + \lambda \times complexity)$<br><br>For more details, refer [google developers](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/overfitting/model-complexity)<br><br>Regularization types:<br>- L1 regularization<br>- L2 regularization<br><br>A regularization rate (lambda) controls the strength of regularization, with higher values leading to simpler models and lower values increasing the risk of overfitting.<br><br>A high regularization rate:<br>- Strengthens the influence of regularization, thereby reducing the chances of overfitting.<br>- Tends to produce a histogram of model weights having the following characteristics:<br>- a normal distribution<br>- a mean weight of 0.<br><br>A low regularization rate:<br>- Lowers the influence of regularization, thereby increasing the chances of overfitting.<br>- Tends to produce a histogram of model weights with a flat distribution.<br> |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
