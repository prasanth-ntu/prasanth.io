## 📋 Key Insights

### Company Highlights
- Founded in 2017 as Memorial Sloan Kettering spin-out
- First FDA-cleared AI application in pathology
- Raised ~$239 million in venture funding
- Serves >1,000 institutions across 45 countries

### Technology Advantages
- **Exclusive Dataset**: >25 million digitized slides from MSK
- **Virchow2G**: 1.8 billion-parameter vision transformer
- **Regulatory First**: First FDA clearance in digital pathology

### Market Position
- Largest clinically curated dataset in the industry
- Strong regulatory moat with FDA clearances
- Global reach with significant institutional adoption
