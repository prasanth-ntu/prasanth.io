> [!SUMMARY] Standard deviation measures how far results spread from the [average value](https://www.yourdictionary.com/articles/math-terms-glossary).
[^3]

Standard deviation is a statistical measurement of the amount a number varies from the average number in a series. 
- A **low standard deviation** means that the data is very closely related to the average, thus very reliable. 
- A **high standard deviation** means that there is a large variance between the data and the statistical average, and is not as reliable.

![[standard deviation.png]][^1]

For a normally distributed curve[^2],
- ~68% of the data falls within 1 SD of the mean (i..e, $\mu \pm 1\sigma$)
- ~95% of the data falls within 2 SD of the mean (i..e, $\mu \pm 2\sigma$)
- ~99.7% of the data falls within 3 SD of the mean (i..e, $\mu \pm 3\sigma$)
