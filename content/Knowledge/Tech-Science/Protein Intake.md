0.8 g/kg/day of protein intake is recommended by the National Academy of Medicine and the World Health Organization[^1][^2].

 [Peter Attia](https://www.ars.usda.gov/ARSUserFiles/80400530/pdf/usual/Usual_Intake_gender_WWEIA_2017_March%202020.pdf) advocates for 2.2 g/kg/day (or 1 g/lb/day) of protein intake in his book Outlive. That translates to almost 3-fold the established recommendation.[^2]

> [!QUOTE]  Regarding the need to increase protein intake 2-3 fold per day, [Stuart Phillips](https://www.vox.com/future-perfect/410565/protein-muscle-gain-weightlifting-plant-based-vegan), a leading expert on protein, energy, and building muscle mass, who is a professor at McMaster University in Canada, [said](https://www.vox.com/future-perfect/410565/protein-muscle-gain-weightlifting-plant-based-vegan) “It’s baloney. But there’s a generation, particularly young men, and now an increasing number of young women, who are absolutely brainwashed by what they hear online.”
