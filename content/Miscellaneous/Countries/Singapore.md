# HDB

In Singapore, the Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA) divides the country into three main “market segments”. These are known as the 
1. Core Central Region (CCR)
	- The CCR comprises postal districts 9, 10, and 11, including Orchard/River Valley, Tanglin/Holland, and Newton/Novena. It also includes the Downtown Core Planning Area, comprising the CBD, Marina Bay, Bugis, Nicoll Highway, and, of course, the CCR also includes Sentosa
2. the Rest of Central Region (RCR)
	- The RCR stretches west from Queenstown and Bukit Timah to Bishan, Braddell, and Toa Payoh in the north, and then east to MacPherson, Eunos, and Marine Parade
3. the Outside Central Region (OCR).
	- The OCR then comprises the four planning regions – East, North East, North, and West.

# Scam

> Scam victims in Singapore lost S$456m in first half of 2025 with almost 20,000 cases reported.[^2]
> The $456.4 million lost between January and June 2025 was a drop from the $522.4 million lost during the same period in 2024.[^2]
> In 2024, scam victims here lost $1.1 billion, a record high in a single year.[^2]

> [!WARNING] In 79 per cent of the cases, scammers did not gain control of the victims’ accounts but manipulated the victims into transferring money. [^2]

# Causeway
As of 2025, about 300,000 people cross the Causeway daily by vehicle, train or foot [^1]. Singapore’s Immigration and Checkpoints Authority said in January last year that [more than 400,000 people](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/woodlands-checkpoint-redevelopment-expansion-2025-clearance-time-traffic-15-minutes-bke-4082301) are expected to use the Causeway daily by 2050.

## Johor Bahru–Singapore Rapid Transit System (RTS)
A 4 km (2.5 mi) long, twin-track [rapid transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_transit "Rapid transit") system crossing the [Strait of Johor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Johor "Strait of Johor") between [Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysia "Malaysia") and [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore "Singapore"). It will link [Johor Bahru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johor_Bahru "Johor Bahru"), Malaysia, at [Bukit Chagar station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bukit_Chagar_RTS_station "Bukit Chagar RTS station"), with the Singapore [MRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_Rapid_Transit_\(Singapore\) "Mass Rapid Transit (Singapore)") at [Woodlands North station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodlands_North_MRT/RTS_station "Woodlands North MRT/RTS station") in [Woodlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodlands,_Singapore). The line is planned to open in December 2026. [^3]
