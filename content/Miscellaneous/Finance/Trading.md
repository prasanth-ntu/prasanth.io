Few interesting stats from this Business Insider blog post, [I went to a day trading meetup and spoke to people with dreams of getting good enough to quit their 9-to-5](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/went-day-trading-meetup-spoke-171501335.html) written on Oct 5 2025.

> [!INFO] There's been an explosion of interest in trading groups, classes, and coaching in recent years.
> - Global search interest for "day trading class" is up 700% the past quarter and hit an all-time high this summer.
> - Search interest in "trading coach" is up 325% over the same timeframe, while interest in "day trading group" is up 572%.
> - On Coursera, enrollment in [online trading courses](https://www.businessinsider.com/guides/learning/best-free-cheap-online-finance-courses?utm_medium=referral&utm_source=yahoo.com) soared 213% from 2019 to 2024.

> [!DANGER] Becoming a successful trader is notoriously hard. 
> A 2020 [study](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3423101) found that 97% of investors who traded for more than 300 days lost money, and less than 1% earned $54 or more a day.
