### Version A: Problem-first hook

I spent 6 months preparing for ML/AI interviews. Somewhere around tab #47 and my third abandoned Notion database, I realized: the problem isn't a lack of resources. It's the absence of a system.

So I built one. And now I'm open-sourcing it.

**ML Interview Prep Kit** — a comprehensive toolkit for ML/AI/Data Science interview preparation:

📚 100+ topics across 14 categories (ML foundations → LLMs → Agentic AI → MLOps)
🧠 Interactive mind map to visualize everything you know (and don't)
📋 Interview tracking templates with 10 design decisions baked in
💼 Career frameworks — offer decision matrix, negotiation tactics, STAR methodology
🤖 AI prompt templates that work with Claude, Gemini, or ChatGPT

Every topic has two layers: a quick-reference table for revision, and a detailed explanation with interview phrasing you can actually say out loud.

The knowledge base isn't a textbook summary — it grew from real interviews. Every question I couldn't answer well became a new section or expansion.

🔗 Live mind map: interview.prasanth.io
🔗 GitHub: github.com/prasanth-ntu/ml-interview-prep-kit

MIT licensed. Fork it, make it yours, and add your own topics.

If you're prepping for ML interviews — or know someone who is — I hope this saves you some of the chaos I went through.

What started as messy interview notes in a markdown file became a 100+ topic knowledge base, an interactive mind map, and an open-source toolkit.
