# Transport
## Electric Train Service (ETS)

All a traveller from Singapore needs to do is head to the Woodlands North [[Singapore#Johor Bahru–Singapore Rapid Transit System (RTS)|RTS]] station to clear immigration on both sides before taking a five-minute ride across the Johor Strait.
Once at Bukit Chagar RTS station, the traveller can walk for less than five minutes to JB Sentral along a sheltered travellator where they can then board the ETS to KL Sentral.
KTM said that the ==journey between Johor Bahru and Kuala Lumpur, for instance, would take between 3.5 and 4.5 hours, depending on type of ETS service the passenger takes== - be it silver, gold, platinum or express - which has different number of stops and varying level of comfort.
