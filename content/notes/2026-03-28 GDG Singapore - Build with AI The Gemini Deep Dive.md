# Multi-Agent Patterns in ADK — Lai Fong Leong

> Agent Development Kit (ADK) empowers developers to get more reliable, sophisticated, multi-step behaviours from generative models. Instead of one complex prompt, ADK lets you build a flow of multiple, simpler agents that collaborate on a problem by dividing the work. In this session, you will first discover how to design specialized task-oriented agent using ADK. After that, you will learn different agent design patterns, from the Sequential pipeline to the Human-in-the-loop design pattern.

# Gemini CLI: From Vibe Coding to Vibe Living within a Terminal — Guan Wang

> The terminal is no longer just for executing commands; it's becoming the brain of your development workflow. In this session, we'll dive deep into the Gemini CLI, Google's open-source AI agent that brings state-of-the-art reasoning directly to your shell. By the end of this session, you'll know how to leverage Gemini CLI that doesn't just help you vibe code, but navigates your entire ecosystem that powers up your life — all in your terminal.
