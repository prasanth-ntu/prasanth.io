# Webinar Notes
- AI ≠ GAI
- **AI Types**
	- Predictive/Deterministic
	- Probabilistic/Generative
- **When do you want each?**
	- Predictive/Deterministic
		- Medication dosing
		- Clinical protocols
		- Vital sign alerts
	- Probabilistic/Generative
		- Documentation assistance
		- Clinical Summaries
- **Predictive AI Examples in Healthcare**
	- Epilepsy detection
	- https://www.mdcalc.com/calc/115/wells-criteria-pulmonary-embolism
	- https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMsa2001090
- **GenAI Examples in Healthcare**
	- AI scribe tool that listens to your conversation and turns it into a note | [Abrdidge](https://www.abridge.com/)
	- A chatbot that takes the patient's history or helps you understand a research paper
