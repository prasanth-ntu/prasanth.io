## My Takeaways

- Just go and get your hands on the OpenClaw.
- One agent for one role. Narrow the scope and the focus. She has nine agents now, all the way from marketing and sales to assistant, and even one for personal assistant and one for work-related assistant.
- Her (Claire's) motto in work is "fast beats right". She inherently optimizes for efficiency and prefers being "first out of the gate" as a competitive advantage.
- Her baseball AI hack for her kid was quite interesting as well.
- Toward the end of the conversation, Lenny brings up a poignant sentiment: "The only people that will remember that you stayed late at work are your kids."
- Claire uses the thought of **who will be at her funeral** as a clarifying lens to prioritize where she spends her mental energy and time.
